Quick answers

What makes a strong password?

Length. Use a phrase of four or more random words, and use a different one for every important account.

Do I need two-step login?

Yes, for email, WhatsApp, banking and social media. Use an authenticator app instead of SMS when you can.

I clicked a suspicious link. What now?

Change the password for that account from a different device, log out of all sessions, and turn on two-step login. If it was a bank page, call your bank.

Is the password checker safe?

It runs entirely in your browser. Still, never type a password you use today into any site. Try a similar one instead.
